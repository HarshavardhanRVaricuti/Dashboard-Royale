What we'd do with $100k

Three actions, 30 days from Jul 1. Most of the budget stays put.

  • Inspect 5 machines $6,000

    M-1158 pays on far more spins than its published math, every month. The other four are the most negative luck results; inspection rules them in or out.

  • One promotion weekend $15,000

    The February slot tournament added +18% coin-in (≈$80k theo) with no dip afterwards.

  • Wayfinding for Back Forty $7,500

    Weak for every theme and down 30% while its walkway was closed: access is the problem.

  • Keep $71,500 71.5%

    Replacing Back Forty machines didn't help (M-1120 → M-5076: $6.00 → $6.39 theo/hr). Extra technicians would recover only $1–4k.
