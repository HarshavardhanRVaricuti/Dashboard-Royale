What we'd do with $100k

Inspect 5 machines ($6,000). M-1158 pays on far more spins than its published math, every month. The other four are the most negative luck results; inspection rules them in or out.

One promotion weekend ($15,000). The February slot tournament added +18% coin-in (≈$80k theo) with no dip afterwards.

Wayfinding for Back Forty ($7,500). The zone is weak for every theme and fell 30% when its walkway closed: access is the problem.

Keep $71,500. Replacing machines in Back Forty didn't help (M-1120 → M-5076: $6.00 → $6.39 theo/hr). Extra technicians would recover only $1–4k.

Cost = quantity × (unit cost + daily cost × max(0, 30 − lead days)) · g_budget_plan

Luck z = (house win − theo) ÷ its standard deviation (Σ bet² × each game's per-spin payout variance); |z| < 2 is ordinary luck. Hit z compares payout-positive spins with the published hit probability; a large value means the game isn't behaving like its math — a lead to inspect, not proof of wrongdoing. Theo per available hour removes luck and downtime: it is demand for the machine where it stands.

Players' net and comps by tier

We do not recommend targeting heavy losers with promotions.

What changed: on May 1 every comp's points price roughly halved (a beer went from ≈114 to ≈53 points); menu value and cost per item did not change. Redemptions rose only ≈8% and comp cost went from 1.11% of tracked theo (Jan) to 1.32% (Jun) with no visible lift in play. Points are earned ≈20× faster than redeemed, so a cheaper price raises the value of the unredeemed balance. Comp cost is counted once (it is inside POS cost of goods).

Question: how much will the floor wager on a given day? Train Jan 1 – Apr 30, holdout May 1 – Jun 30 (time split; both forecasts frozen before testing). Baseline: constant training-period daily mean. Model: training mean for that weekday. Variant: weekday mean of no-event days × average training-period event uplift, applied only to events announced in advance. No holdout actuals or later-published tourism data are inputs.

Decision it changes: Fri–Sun run ≈30–60% above Tue–Wed and pre-announced events add ≈18% more, so technician coverage and the funded promotion weekend go on Fri–Sun — and the frozen forecast is the counterfactual for measuring the promotion's lift.
