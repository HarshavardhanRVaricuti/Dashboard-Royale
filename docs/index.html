Definitions
Coin-in
Total amount wagered.
House win
Coin-in minus payouts. Swings with luck.
Theo
Expected win: bet × (1 − return to player). Luck-free.
Hold
House win ÷ coin-in (theo hold: theo ÷ coin-in). Ratio of sums.
Luck z
(House win − theo) ÷ its standard deviation. −2 to +2 is ordinary luck.
Hit z
Paying spins vs the published hit rate, in standard deviations.
Theo / available hour
Demand for a machine where it stands, free of luck and downtime.
Carded play
Play tracked on a loyalty card (≈66% of coin-in).

Q3 · How much did machine downtime cost, and why did it happen?
Finding

About 5,000 down hours cost at most ≈$90k of theo in six months. Ridgeline machines fail 2–4× as often; day-shift technicians ran short in Apr–May and waits rose. Too small to justify paying for extra staff.

How to read

Lost theo values each down hour at that machine's own theo rate, so it is an upper bound (players may move to another machine). Use Zone or Bank to isolate one area.

Q5 · What did the comp program cost, and what changed?
Finding

On May 1 the points price of every comp roughly halved. Comp cost rose from 1.11% of tracked theo (Jan) to 1.32% (Jun) with no visible lift in play.

How to read

The left chart shows points needed per item (a beer went from ≈114 to ≈53 points; its value and cost did not change). Points are earned ≈20× faster than redeemed, so a cheaper price raises the value of the unredeemed balance. Comp cost is counted once (it is inside POS cost of goods).

Model · Can daily coin-in be forecast better than a simple baseline?
Finding

Yes. On the unseen May–Jun holdout, a weekday-mean forecast cuts the average daily error from $218k to $69k; adding pre-announced events brings it to $51k.

Method

Train Jan 1 – Apr 30, holdout May 1 – Jun 30 (shaded); every forecast is frozen before the holdout. Baseline: constant training mean. Model: training mean for that weekday. Variant: weekday mean of no-event days × average event uplift, for events announced in advance. No holdout actuals or later tourism data are inputs.

Decision

Fri–Sun run ≈30–60% above Tue–Wed and pre-announced events add ≈18% more, so technician coverage and the funded promotion weekend go on Fri–Sun, and the frozen forecast is the counterfactual for measuring the promotion's lift.
